Deployment Incident Guide

If a problem begins immediately after a deployment:

1. Review deployment logs for errors or failed configuration steps.
2. Compare the new release configuration with the previous stable release.
3. Check application health checks and dependent service status.
4. Identify how many customers or users are affected.
5. Roll back the release if the impact is high and the root cause cannot be resolved quickly.
6. Record the incident timeline and the final resolution.
